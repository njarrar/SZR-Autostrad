Drive the real ring roads of five cities in 3D: Amman, Dubai, Riyadh, Nairobi and Johannesburg.

FIVE CITIES, REAL ROADS
• Amman Loop: 22 km over the hills, past Abdali, the King Abdullah I Mosque, the Citadel, the Raghadan Flagpole, the Roman Theatre, Abdoun Bridge and the 7th Circle.
• Dubai Loop: 47 km at sunset down Sheikh Zayed Road, past the Dubai Frame, the Museum of the Future, Burj Khalifa, the Marina, Ain Dubai and Burj Al Arab.
• Riyadh Loop: 43 km from Masmak Fort and the old town, up King Fahd Road past Al Faisaliah and Kingdom Centre, out to old Diriyah.
• Nairobi Loop: 32 km on the left side of the road, past KICC, Uhuru Park, Nairobi National Park, The Junction and Westlands, in a matatu with its own art.
• Joburg Loop: 46 km on the left, past the Carlton Centre, the Nelson Mandela Bridge, Ponte City, Sandton, Soweto's Orlando Towers and FNB Stadium, in a minibus taxi.

Each city has its own look: white stone houses stacked up Amman's hills, Dubai's wall of glass towers and Metro, Riyadh's mud brick walls and palms, Nairobi's jacarandas and tin roofs, and Joburg's mine dumps and skyline.

DRIVE LIKE A LOCAL
• Pick a trip between landmarks and get there without crashing.
• Weave past traffic for points, and chain close passes for more.
• Watch for speed cameras, speed bumps and tolls. Fines are tiered, like the real ones.
• Drive a taxi, a matatu or the Coaster bus and pick people up at the stops, or take the Formula 1 car for the fastest run.
• Fill up at fuel stations, fire the nitro and honk the horn.
• An ambulance, a wedding convoy or a tailgater can turn up on the road.

KIDS MODE
A gentle way to play for ages 4 to 7, one tap from the menu.
• Pick a car and a place from big pictures. A voice reads each question.
• The car drives itself. Steer with two big arrows or a big steering wheel.
• Catch stars and balloons on the way.
• Bumps only go BOING. No fines, no damage, no game over.
• Every trip ends with stars, confetti and a sticker for the album.
• A grown-up holds the lock for three seconds to leave.
